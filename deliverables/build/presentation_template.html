Problem

Fees, career rules, admission and the regional scholarship are described in Italian documents. Missing a deadline excludes a student from the scholarship (Art. 10 of the call). General chatbots answer fluently but do not show their source and can invent dates.

Research question

How well can search over official documents, a translation step and a verified language-model answer serve questions asked in many languages, and when must the system say "I don't know"?

Limits

Non-Italian questions are machine translations; the ground truth misses some relevant passages; the verifier sometimes removes too much; one university, one call.

Next steps

Questions written by real international students and checked by native speakers; human review of answers; more documents after licence checks; per-language "I don't know" thresholds.

Use of AI tools: a coding assistant for code and documents; a language model for translations and answers. Declared in the report.
